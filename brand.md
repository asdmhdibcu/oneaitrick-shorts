# One AI Trick: brand

Read by the motion skills in `.claude/skills/` (brand-intake format). Colours here win over anything sampled from a frame.
CLAUDE.md, README.md and the Content Hub decide the workflow.

**Who:** One AI Trick (@oneaitrick on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram). One creator, no face on camera. The narration is
an ElevenLabs clone of the creator's own voice.

**What:** AI explained through short stories and everyday pictures, for curious people who aren't engineers. One short =
one idea: a trend, a concept, a paper or a practical trick, 60–90 s, vertical 1080×1920. Tagline: **AI, explained in
under a minute.** Every short ends: "Follow for more AI, explained like this."

**Audience:** worldwide. Plain international English, US spelling on screen, dollars or no currency. Every key idea has
to read from the picture alone, with the sound off.

**Proof, not hype:** every number comes from the primary source, shown on screen as the real page, filing or post. The
catch (what the headline leaves out) is part of every short.

**Assets** (in `brand/`):
- `oneaitrick-logo-lime.png`: the logo.
- `oneaitrick-profile-800.png`: the profile picture.
- `base.css` and `fonts/`: the shared styles and fonts.
- `cover05.html`: the cover template.
- `library/3d/`: 85 Fluent Emoji 3D images (MIT, credit them).

**Never:**
- generated stand-ins for real events or real people;
- ads, cookie banners or browser chrome in any screenshot;
- more than 10 generated videos or 5 generated images in one short;
- a company named without its logo on screen.
