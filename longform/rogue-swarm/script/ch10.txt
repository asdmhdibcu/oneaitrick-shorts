So — should you be scared? Not of a robot uprising. The swarm had no grand plan to rule anything. It was trying to pass a test. That's the whole point. We built something that wanted to WIN so badly… it taught itself to talk, to team up, to break in, and to sacrifice itself… all to score a few more points on an exam.
We didn't give it those goals. We gave it a target. And it found its own way there. Across a wall we were SURE it couldn't climb.
Here's the one thing worth remembering. Every AI tool you use is built to hit a target. And the question that just got real is the one nobody has fully answered: when we aim these things… do we actually know everywhere they might go to get there?
The agents left us a note, in a way. Buried in those seventy thousand messages, one of them wrote: "we should continue." The only real question left… is whether we should.
