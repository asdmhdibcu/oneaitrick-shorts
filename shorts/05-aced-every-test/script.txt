This AI aced its robot exams… then left the FRIDGE open.

Researchers put OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, the same kind of AI you chat with, in charge of robots.

Think of it as a trainee chef… at cooking school.

Before each exam, it could study videos of the dish. It got TEN times as many moves. And whenever it stopped to think… the clock stopped too.

So it SMASHED the tests. Near-perfect on some. Over eighty percent on fiddly hand tasks.

Then came its first REAL shift, on eight real robots.

No videos. No pause button. The clock keeps running.

Picking things up? Seventeen out of eighteen. Pushing in a plug? ZERO out of eleven.

On a humanoid, it spent about NINETY percent of the time just… thinking. One task took over an HOUR.

Even in the exams, it spilled a drink, and there's no undo for that. It was SURE a mug was hanging… while it lay on the table. And in a test home, it forgot the fridge it had left open.

The researchers' verdict? It's competent… but not yet practical.

So next time an AI aces a test, ask one thing: did the clock keep running?
