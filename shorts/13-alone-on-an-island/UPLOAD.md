## YouTube Shorts (English, default)

**Title:** Scientists left an AI alone on an island. It started to play.

**Description:**

MIT left 13 copies of an AI alone on a virtual island for 30 hours. No task. No reward. It invented games, built a
temple, lost a golden ring and made it a heart of stones. Then the twist: the ones that played did about 3× better at
real work.

Is it really playing? Even the scientists aren't sure. But the lesson works for us: play isn't wasted time.

Paper: Cloos, Norelli, Durbin, Andreas, Rus & Isola, "Is this machine playing?" (MIT CSAIL, 2026), arXiv 2610.07130.
