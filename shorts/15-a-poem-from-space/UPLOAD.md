## YouTube Shorts (English, default)

**Title:** Google sent an AI to space. Its first words were a poem.

**Description:**

Google put one of its AI chips on a fridge-sized satellite and launched it on a SpaceX rocket. The first thing the AI
said back from orbit was a haiku about space (the team asked for one). Why send AI to space at all? AI needs more and
more electricity, and in the right orbit a solar panel gets sun almost all the time: up to 8 times more power than on
Earth.

But space is hard: no air to carry heat away, radiation that flips bits, lasers that must hit a coin-sized target from
far away, and launches that are still too expensive. Even the project's leader says it won't be cheaper than Earth for
years. Every question you ask an AI costs power, and the search for more just left the planet.
