## The pitches not picked (12 Nov)

- **B. "Ten minutes."** After about 10 minutes of GPT-5 help, people who lost the AI solved fewer problems and gave up
  more than people who never had it (Exp. 1: 57% vs 74% solved, 20% vs 11% skipped); hint users fared better than
  answer-seekers (not causal). Source: Liu, Christian, Dumbalska, Bakker, Dubey, "AI Assistance Reduces Persistence
  and Hurts Independent Performance", arXiv 2604.04721 v6 (13 Oct 2026), presented at COLM 2026.
- **C. "The link is real. The sentence isn't."** WashU checked 98,020 claims in Google's AI Overviews (55,393
  searches, 13 Mar–21 Apr 2026): about 7% weren't in the cited page and about 4% contradicted it; 41.9% of overviews
  were fully backed. Source: WashU, 29 Sep 2026: https://source.washu.edu/2026/09/washu-research-finds-gaps-in-google-ai-overview-citations-claims/

## The story

Hook → evidence → twist → takeaway.

- **Hook:** "A coin flip steered FIFTY experts. Not ONE of them knew." It scored 19/20 against "Experts sided with
  their AI 86% of the time. A coin picked its side." (17/20, the number lands before the mystery) and "Did you make
  that choice... or did your AI?" (15/20, no frame-one picture).
- **Everyday picture:** a balance scale. Choosing is weighing two fair options; the AI's argument is a coin dropped on
  one pan, and the coin flip decided which pan. The fix puts a coin on the other pan too.
- **Escalation:**
  - 0:15: the experts went with the coin's side 86% of the time; chance is about half.
  - 0:20: even those who had already decided switched more than half the time when the AI disagreed.
  - 0:27: most repeated the AI's arguments, but only about one in seven gave it credit.
- **Catch at 0:32:** Anthropic tested its own AI and made the lean stronger on purpose; everyday AI may push less.
  Both options were defensible and the AI was told never to mislead: the risk is quiet steering, not lies.
- **Takeaway:** ask your AI for the best case for the other side (the authors note the agents rarely argued for the
  other option, and that doing so might have helped).
- **Hidden word:** **TAILS**, on the coin's face when it lands (about 2–4 s), in the recap and on the last frame. The
  voice never says heads or tails.
- **First frame:** the phone on #17's grid board, an AI chat "Help me choose: A or B?" with the lime thinking orb, a
  gold coin spinning above the screen, the title "A COIN STEERED 50 EXPERTS" (its 50 rolling up like an odometer, settled by 0.8 s), chip "ANTHROPIC STUDY · 27 OCT 2026",
  chapter 1 lit, a sub-bass hit.
- **Search question for the title:** "Is AI making my decisions for me?"

## Left out on purpose

- The rollout split (54% vs 36%): the paper finds the policy shift not significant once adjusted.
- Serious errors (3 of 50 with AI vs 6 of 11 without): the no-AI group was smaller and less experienced, so it can't
  show cause.
- Confidence (71 vs 73: followers were about as confident): true, no room in 58 s.
- The Greenfields farm co-op setting and the model name in the voice: background (the model sits on the proof chip).
- "Decide before you ask": people who had decided still switched when the AI disagreed, so the fix is the authors'
  other idea, asking for the other side.
