## YouTube Shorts (English, default)

**Title:** Does using AI make you give up faster? 10 minutes was enough

**Description:**

Ten minutes of AI help... and people gave up almost twice as often once it was taken away.

In a peer-reviewed study (COLM 2026), 1,222 adults did math (fractions) or reading problems, about half of them with an
AI helper (GPT-5) in a sidebar. After about 10 minutes the AI was taken away, in the first experiment without warning.
Alone, the people who had the AI solved fewer problems than people who never had it (57 vs 74 out of 100 in fractions)
and skipped almost twice as often (20 vs 11 in 100). In reading: 8 skips in 100 against 1. The researchers think AI
trains us to expect instant answers, so we stop working through hard problems on our own.

People who used the AI mainly for hints did about as well as people with no AI (76 vs 77 out of 100). The catch: these
were short tests, taken straight after, so nobody knows yet whether the effect lasts or adds up, and the hint result is
a clue, not proof, because people chose how to use the AI. Still, it's an easy thing to try: ask your AI for a hint
first, not the answer.

Source:
Liu, Christian, Dumbalska, Bakker, Dubey, "AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance",
COLM 2026 (Carnegie Mellon, UC Berkeley, Oxford, MIT, UCLA): https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721
