- **Catch:** these are educated guesses. The Economist itself says the loudest AI fears (jobs, water and power) lack evidence so far.
- **Takeaway:** the trends that touch most people are the quiet ones: interest rates, fertilizer, a cheaper pill. Comment which one matters most to you.
- **Politics:** told neutrally. The Economist's opinions are attributed to it, and each side gets one line (Le Pen, Trump).
- **Hidden word:** GUESSWORK, on the title card under the 2027 title. Never said.
